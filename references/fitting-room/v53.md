# V53 — first-event saved-look pointer

Production QA found the first pointermove could hit the canvas before the hover rule enabled the saved-view overlay. This left the arrow at its default center and the square visible until the next movement.

Desktop-only fix: `.saved-screen .saved-card .saved-view{pointer-events:auto}`. The action has a full card-body hit area before hover. Its higher-specificity export-active rule still disables the action during export. No SVG geometry, colors, cursor smoothing or click behavior changed.
